# Cookies Policy

## 1. What are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files that are downloaded to your computer when you access certain websites. They allow the website to recognize your preferences and provide a more personalized experience.

## 2. How do we use Cookies?
Multtiplus uses cookies to:
- Ensure the proper functioning of the website (Necessary Cookies).
- Analyze how visitors use the website to improve performance (Analysis Cookies).
- Remember your language preferences and other settings (Functionality Cookies).

## 3. Types of Cookies we use

### Necessary Cookies
These cookies are essential for you to navigate the website and use its features. Without these cookies, the requested services cannot be provided.

### Analysis Cookies (Analytics)
We use tools like Google Analytics to collect information about how visitors interact with the website. This information is aggregated and anonymous, helping us understand which pages are most popular and how we can improve content.

### Functionality Cookies
These cookies allow the website to remember choices you make (such as your username, language, or the region you are in) and provide enhanced, more personal features.

## 4. How to manage Cookies?
You can control and/or delete cookies as you wish. You can delete all cookies already installed on your computer and configure most browsers to prevent cookie installation. However, if you do this, you may have to manually adjust some preferences every time you visit a website, and some services and functionalities may not work.

To learn more about how to manage cookies in your browser, check the links below:
- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/enable-and-disable-cookies-website-preferences)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/manage-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-view-allow-block-delete-and-use-168dab11-0753-043d-7c16-ede5947fc64d)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT201265)

## 5. Changes to this Policy
We may update our Cookies Policy periodically. We recommend that you check this page regularly to be aware of any updates.
