# Privacy Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** February 17, 2026

Multtiplus values user privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share your information, in compliance with the General Personal Data Protection Law (LGPD).

## 1. Collected Data

We collect only the data necessary to provide our services and improve your experience.

The following data may be collected:
- Full name;
- Email address;
- Phone number;
- Company name;
- Technical navigation information (cookies, IP address).

## 2. Purpose of Data Use

We use your data for the following purposes:
- Responding to contact requests and quotes;
- Providing contracted services;
- Sending institutional communications and updates;
- Complying with legal and regulatory obligations;
- Improving site security and operation.

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal data. Your data may be shared only in the following situations:
- With suppliers and partners essential for service provision;
- When required by law or competent authority;
- To protect Multtiplus rights in disputes.

## 4. Data Security

We adopt technical and administrative measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or destruction. We use encryption and secure protocols.

## 5. Data Retention

We will retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, contractual, accountability, or competent authority requisition obligations.

## 6. Your Rights (LGPD)

You have the following rights regarding your data:
- Confirmation of processing existence;
- Access to data;
- Correction of incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data;
- Anonymization, blocking, or elimination of unnecessary data;
- Data portability;
- Elimination of data processed with consent;
- Revocation of consent.

## 7. Data Protection Officer (DPO)

To exercise your rights or ask questions about this Policy, contact our Data Protection Officer:
